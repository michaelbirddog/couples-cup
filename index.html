COUPLES CUP

Every couple plays every other couple once in each game. Double elimination cannot do that with 5 teams, so this is a dual round-robin with optional championship finals.

Format locked in: 10 time slots. Each slot has one match of Game 1 and one match of Game 2, plus one couple sitting out. That is 20 matches so every pair meets in both games. After the round-robin you can play 1st vs 2nd in each game.
